# Goldcom Design DNA: from studio references to procedural motion

Research basis: homepages plus 2–3 case studies each for Tendril, ManvsMachine, BUCK, DIA, Giant Ant and Ordinary Folk, and featured pieces on Stash and Motionographer, all scraped on 2026-10-24. Every claim about how the work looks comes from stills that were viewed (full-page screenshots, not stored in the repo). Claims about how the work moves come from case-study text, loop and GIF file names (e.g. `MP10_LOOP_GLASS`, `CB1_RND_LiquidGrow`) and frame sequences. I could not play the videos in this environment. Where a point is my own production rule rather than something I observed, it says so.

Access notes: Tendril's Coinbase CB1 page could not be captured, so that project is analysed from its case-study text only.

---

## 0. The five-line summary

1. **The object is the message.** Every strong reference explains a financial idea with *one physical thing doing one thing*: a Newton's cradle for balance (Tendril/Questrade), a candlestick chart that becomes a hill someone walks on (MVSM/Robinhood), a coin stack seen through one open tile in a dark wall (BUCK/J.P. Morgan). Words come after the picture, or not at all.
2. **Material and light do the selling.** Metal and frosted glass on a near-black field, lit by a single soft key and a rim, with *one* accent colour glowing from inside. This one recipe is how Tendril, BUCK and Studio River all make their work look expensive.
3. **One accent, one hero, one line.** No reference frame has more than one focal object, more than one accent colour or more than one line of display text.
4. **Motion = mass.** Premium work moves heavy things slowly and lands them decisively, with no rubbery bounce. The camera never fully stops, and that is what makes it feel "live".
5. **Rhythm is composed, not added.** DIA designs to time signatures. Tendril and Hublot cut to sound design (Cypher Audio, Wave Studios). Key events land on beats.

## 1. Studio by studio

### 1.1 Tendril (Toronto, part of the Forever group)
**Signature:** photoreal CG of materials and mechanisms. Desaturated, near-monochrome, macro, precise. They make invisible engineering tangible ("we make it impossible to miss").

**Work I looked at**
- **Questrade rebrand (3D + motion system, 2026).** The brief was to explain finance "without relying on a conventional fintech visual language or words to explain them". Their answer was *"The Physics of Finance"*: products shown as physical instruments that "click, turn, swing and find their place", exploring "gravity, tilt, rhythm and balance". Stills I saw: a **spiral ramp of brushed-metal slabs rising like a bar chart, with mint-green light glowing from inside the tallest slabs** (`refs/tendril-2`); a **card chip in brushed steel floating above a stack of frosted-glass and metal blocks with green light leaking through the seams** (`refs/tendril-3`); a Newton's cradle of glass discs; a chrome fan or wave of discs; a "Connected" toggle sitting on glass tiles. Each product got a 1:1 social loop (QT_Stocks&ETFs, QT_Borrow, QT_Spend&Save, QT_Trade, QT_Plus). One R&D frame is literally named "GoldTester".
- **Coinbase CB1 card, "Made by Metal".** "Abstract elements coalesce into form layer by layer… data becomes matter, concept becomes form." The embossed genesis block is a detail revealed "with cinematic precision". R&D GIFs: *LiquidGrow*, *Rock*, *MRI*, *TypeReveal*, *Assembly*. Sound by Wave Studios.
- **Hublot MP-10.** "Teasing each intricate component with texture, but without fully revealing the masterpiece until you're at the edge of your seat." Separate seamless loops: LOOP_TECH, LOOP_POWER (two **white-gold blocks sliding on a vertical axis**), LOOP_GLASS. Graphic overlays annotate the engineering. Houdini simulations, sound by Cypher Audio. The hero still is a black void with a top light falling off into film grain and the mechanism emerging from darkness.
- Homepage hero: an extreme macro of a robot joint with very shallow depth of field. Gloss black against woven fabric is the only contrast.

**Techniques:** macro CG with shallow depth of field; brushed (anisotropic) metal next to frosted glass; accent light *emitted from inside* objects rather than painted on; mechanisms used as metaphors; seamless 1:1 loops per idea; graphic hairline annotations over CG; slow reveal-by-parts.
**Colour:** near-black green-grey (#141815-ish) or pale sage, chrome greys, one accent (Questrade mint, Coinbase blue). The accent covers under 5% of the frame.
**Type:** almost absent in the films. On social cards the type is small and calm, in a grotesk set at 1–2 sizes, and the object carries the frame.
**Camera:** long lens, low angles, macro inserts, slow push-ins and slides along an object. Rack focus is used as the reveal.
**Pacing and transitions:** patient. Shots hold. Transitions are match-cuts on form, or one object assembling into the next.
**Sound to motion:** mechanical ticks, clicks and metallic resonance drive each micro-event (dedicated audio houses on every project).

### 1.2 ManvsMachine (London / LA)
**Signature:** design-led direction. A bold, simple idea executed crisply, mixing CG, live action and graphics. Comfortable both in flat saturated colour fields and in photoreal tactility (the Moncler quilted-puffer world).

**Work I looked at**
- **Robinhood "Future Trading" (33 s brand relaunch).** "Fusing trading UI, kinetic brand graphics and live action." Key frame: **a candlestick chart becomes the silhouette of a hill, and a lone figure walks along its ridge** against a deep-blue gradient sky, with a hairline price axis on the right and a tiny "Limit / Stop / +$43.14" chip (`refs/mvsm-2`). Others: a minimal ticker HUD ("BTC ▲ $3.65 0.19%") floating over a motion-blurred field; a **macro eye**; a **watch movement** (a cut-in for precision); a single water ripple; a dot-grid "starfield" around a man lit by his phone (`refs/mvsm-3`). The grade is cold teal-navy.
- **Trade Every Play (Robinhood):** a baseball runner with thin tracking lines and a tiny data label ("ACC 6.88 m/s").
- Homepage grid: big two-line titles ("Gilga Farm / for Moncler") set in a clean grotesk directly over full-bleed imagery (`refs/mvsm-1`).

**Techniques:** *data as landscape* (chart → terrain); HUD annotation in 1 px lines and 12–16 px labels; macro inserts (eye, watch) as rhythmic punctuation between wide shots; restrained live-action lighting with a single key and deep shadow.
**Colour:** one grade per film; UI colours (green and red) only inside the tiny HUD chips.
**Type:** large clean grotesk for titles, very small for data. The scale gap between them is roughly 10:1.
**Camera:** wide lonely compositions with the subject at about 10% of frame area and huge negative space, cut against extreme macros. That alternation is the rhythm.
**Pacing:** 30-second spots with roughly 1–2 s shots; the edit is cut to music (Zelig).

### 1.3 BUCK (global)
**Signature:** range and systems, from cel animation to premium CG. They build visual *systems* (palette, linework, imagery, motion rules) rather than one-off spots.

**Work I looked at**
- **J.P. Morgan Payments creative expression** (the most directly relevant reference for Goldcom).
  - Palette: the JPM brown expanded into shades, plus clementine, topaz and blue gradients. Warm, earthy, premium.
  - Type: Amplitude (geometric sans) paired with Celeste (serif). Stat frame: **a huge serif "$150M" with a tiny "Numbers" label inside a hairline frame on cream**.
  - Linework device: "straight lines, circles, and squares" as metaphors for connections. Hairlines draw over photography and CG.
  - Imagery: "metals and glass that can take on a glow effect… contrasted with woods and golds". Behaviours are "layering, activating, emerging or revealing".
  - Stills: **glass discs with glowing inner rings rolling along brushed-bronze rails** (`refs/buck-2`); **a glass coin beside a gold bell-curve sculpture made of layered translucent ribbons on a plaster-sand surface with golden-hour raking light** (`refs/buck-3`); **a coin stack revealed through one opened tile in a wall of dark bronze panels** (`refs/buck-4`); wooden pucks with glowing gold seams; a spiral of white fins.
  - Motion rule: "connected through a patient and fluid rhythm and cadence, deliberately revealing information to viewers in a subtle and focused manner."
- **LinkedIn Celebrations:** 16 animated loops, *one metaphor per milestone* (climbers on giant leaves, POV bicycle handlebars, a bouquet passed hand to hand, binoculars). Grainy textures, blended gradients, a consistent touch of brand blue. Delivered as Lottie: "built a texture sheet from a 3-PNG sequence to reduce JSON size". Proof that premium feel survives lightweight, code-driven delivery.

**Techniques:** a warm material palette (bronze, wood, glass, plaster); raking light with long soft shadows; hairline geometry overlays; a reveal device (tile opens, layer slides); a serif/sans contrast for numbers.
**Camera:** three-quarter top-down "tabletop" views with a long lens; slow lateral slides.
**Pacing:** slow, with holds long enough to read. Fluid transitions where each element hands off to the next.

### 1.4 DIA (design, research and innovation; New York, now Switzerland)
**Signature:** kinetic typography and identity systems. Type *is* the image, and motion is designed in from the start rather than applied to a static layout.

**Work and sources I looked at**
- **Coinbase Onchain Summer (2023):** a "generative dynamic system referencing links and the Base logo", applied to modular layouts and motion toolkits for social, web, print and wild postings. Stills: **ultra-condensed grotesk filling the frame edge to edge**; letterforms rebuilt as **dotted outlines** (type made of dots that can animate along their paths); fields of circles; concentric target rings; four flat colours (violet, yellow, cyan, pink) (`refs/dia-2`).
- The studio index (`refs/dia-1`) is a wall of mostly black-and-white typographic experiments: stretched, repeated, extruded and masked type.
- Interviews: Mitch Paone treats design like jazz composition. "We break our (moving) design down into time signatures, rhythmic patterns and progressions… the movement is baked directly into the design… Everything is very choreographed." For Squarespace, the motion came from the spoken cadence of the name: "Square" is a 2D beat and "Space" is a 3D beat seen through isometric camera moves. He warns that variable-font animation "usually end[s] up with… a bouncy-ball effect".

**Techniques:** type set at frame-filling scale (cap height 40–70% of the frame); repetition and stretch; dotted or outlined construction; generative layouts on a modular grid; motion derived from content and sound.
**Colour:** flat and limited (2–4 colours) or pure black and white.
**Pacing:** strictly metric. Changes land on the beat and patterns loop in phrases of 4 or 8.
**Relevance to Goldcom:** the "text" in a frame should *be* the visual (one giant number or word doing something), not a stack of three lines.

### 1.5 Giant Ant (Vancouver)
**Signature:** handmade craft. Painterly cel animation and illustration with wit, plus CG when the product needs it.

**Work I looked at**
- **Koho "Metal" (20 s origin story for a metal card):** "shiny, heavy, forged of the finest metal a computer can render." Stills: **a blue-violet brushed-metal card bursting out of rock and concrete with debris in the air** (`refs/giantant-2`); macro of the embossed logo and chamfered edge; **the card balanced on dark rocks against a purple gradient** (`refs/giantant-3`); R&D "Concrete" tests of a metal blade cutting into stone. Sound by Jeff Moberg.
- **OpenTable "Find a Restaurant" (2026 campaign):** hand-drawn cel with a **3–4 colour palette (oxblood or mauve, burnt orange, near-black, one hot-pink accent)**. Heavy foreground silhouettes frame the action, warm pools of light, close-ups of hands, glasses and candles. It focuses on "the *reasons* we gather" rather than food photography.
- **Optimizely "Mark Eting":** surreal flat illustration on acid green (`refs/giantant-1`).

**Techniques:** a material *origin story* (the product forged from raw matter); heavy objects with debris and contact; cel animation with limited palettes and silhouette framing; the emotion of the moment rather than the product.
**Colour:** strict limitation, with one accent used for the "spark" (pink lipstick, a red menu).
**Camera:** intimate close-ups, over-the-shoulder silhouettes, low angles on product.

### 1.6 Ordinary Folk (Vancouver)
**Signature:** "transforming concepts into motion". Explainers with strong geometric design: clean, warm and witty 2D characters, with some 3D.

**Work I looked at**
- **CryptoCubes (1:57, 2D + 3D):** **an entirely monochrome warm-grey world of isometric cubes**. One face of each form carries a noise or grain texture; the rest is flat shading. Cube-to-cube transitions ("Cube_Transition_Base_Master") and folding-in-on-itself studies. Rare frames add a single acid-green accent (`refs/ordinaryfolk-2`).
- **Twilio Trusted Communications (2D characters, 1:52):** **a limited palette of navy, red, light blue and pink skin**; flat characters with soft gradients on screens; circular vignette framing; a radial "tunnel" composition; thin squiggle lines as energy accents (`refs/ordinaryfolk-3`). Music and SFX by Ambrose Yu.
- Homepage: a big geometric sans and generous white space (`refs/ordinaryfolk-1`).

**Techniques:** a single geometric primitive as the whole world (cube, sphere, circle); circular masks to focus attention; limited palettes; transitions built from the primitive itself (a cube unfolds into the next scene).
**Pacing:** explainer cadence, with one idea per 3–5 s scene and voice-over driving the cuts.

### 1.7 Stash (curation)

- **Studio River, Hublot spec "R.EVISITED":** black and white. A watch is trapped under Arctic ice while a translucent jellyfish-like creature circles it; the ice fractures and the watch rises to the surface. "Each frame magnifies stillness." ### 1.8 Motionographer (curation)
- **Chris Ullens, Diptyque "Médicis":** stop-motion miniature artisans pour wax into moulds in a softly lit arched workshop. ## 2. Principles that separate studio-grade work from AI-template work

### 2.1 Idea and copy
- **One idea per post. One sentence explains it. The visual alone should make the point with the sound off and the text hidden.** Test: cover the text. If the frame still says "you can start with 1 g", it passes.
- **Text budget per frame:** at most one display line (Arabic ≤ 6 words, English ≤ 7), plus optionally one caption (price stamp, legal or handle) at the caption size. No kicker + headline + subline stacks.
- **Language:** Arabic-first. Do not stack bilingual copy in one frame; make AR and EN variants, or keep English to a 60%-size secondary line. Pick one digit system (Western 0–9 or Eastern ٠–٩) for prices across the whole brand and never mix them.
- **Numbers are heroes.** A price or a "1 g" can carry a frame by itself, BUCK-style: a numeral at 200–320 px with a 24–28 px label (a scale ratio of at least 8:1).

### 2.2 Composition (at 1080 px width)
- The hero object fills **35–55% of frame height**. Leave **40–60% of the frame as quiet negative space**: a soft light pool on onyx, never a pattern.
- Put the focal point on a third or slightly below centre. Text sits in the opposite third. Mirrored compositions are fine for logo stings only.
- **4:5 feed (1080×1350):** keep critical content inside the central **1012 px** width, because the profile grid crops to 3:4.
- **9:16 Reels and Stories:** keep text out of the top ~220 px and bottom ~420 px, and out of the right ~120 px where the UI buttons sit.
- Alternate scale between frames, MVSM-style: a wide lonely shot (subject at ~10% of the frame) followed by an extreme macro. Two consecutive frames at the same scale feel templated.

### 2.3 Typography
- At most two weights per frame (e.g. IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400 + 700). No outlines, glows, bevels or gradient fills on text.
- **Scale contrast:** display to caption at least **4:1**, stat to label at least **8:1**. Use tabular figures for anything that changes (`TYPE.price.tnum`).
- **Arabic motion rule:** animate Arabic **by word or by line, never by letter**, because per-letter animation breaks the joined script. Reveal right to left with a mask wipe, using the snap ease below with 60–90 ms between words. Numerals can roll digit by digit (odometer) because digits are not joined.
- Leading: Arabic 1.18 (as the brand kit says) with generous tracking on Latin captions only.

### 2.4 Material and light (three.js recipe for gold)
- **Gold is reflections, not a yellow fill.** Use `MeshPhysicalMaterial`, metalness 1, base colour ≈ linear (1.0, 0.77, 0.34) (sRGB around #F2C66E; nudge toward brand gold #DBA761 for 24K warmth). Roughness **0.12–0.20** for polished minted faces and **0.30–0.45 with anisotropy 0.6–0.8** for brushed or cast faces. Add a subtle noise roughness map (±0.03), because perfect CG gold looks fake.
- **Environment does 80% of the work.** Build a PMREM environment from a *black room with 2–3 emissive rectangles*: a large soft top box, a thin vertical strip for a crisp edge highlight, and a dim warm bounce card. Do not use outdoor HDRIs or default RoomEnvironment brightness. This is what gives gold its black-and-white specular banding (Tendril, Studio River).
- Lighting: one key from top-back, a rim from the side, and a near-black fill. Light direction must be identical across every element in the frame.
- Ground: graphite or onyx stone with roughness 0.45–0.6 for a soft reflection, plus a contact shadow (blurred AO plane) under every object. Nothing floats without a shadow unless floating is the point.
- Accent light comes from *inside* or *behind* objects (Questrade green seams, BUCK glowing rings). For Goldcom, use warm spark #F2D46B emissive at low intensity, never on more than 5% of the frame.
- Tone mapping: AgX or ACES, exposure 0.9–1.1. Post: bloom threshold 0.85–0.95, strength 0.15–0.3 (only speculars bloom); film grain 3–5%; vignette 0.25–0.35; DOF focused on the hero. No lens flares, and chromatic aberration only up to 0.5 px.
- Camera: **FOV 18–30°** (long lens) for product and **12–18°** for macro. Never the default 50–75°.

### 2.5 Motion (my production rules, consistent with the references' "patient, fluid, weighted" behaviour)
| Token | Curve | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| settle | cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) | 700–1100 ms | heavy object arrives, panel opens |
| lift | cubic-bezier(0.64, 0, 0.78, 0) | 250–400 ms | exits, object leaving frame |
| glide | cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1) | whole loop | camera, light sweeps |
| snap | cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.1, 1) | 350–500 ms | type, UI, numbers |
| weight spring | stiffness 200, damping 26, mass 1.2 | — | landing with ≤3% overshoot |

- **Motion = mass:** gold is dense (19.3 g/cm³), so it accelerates slowly and stops decisively. No elastic easing, no squash on metal, overshoot of 3% or less.
- **Live-feeling:** the camera never fully stops. Keep a drift of 0.5–1.5° per second of orbit or a 2–5% dolly across the loop. Specular highlights crawl as the light sweeps. Text is the only thing allowed to be static.
- **Stagger:** words 60–90 ms apart; numeral digits 25–40 ms; instanced arrays (grids of bars) by distance from the origin at 8–15 ms per unit.
- **Holds:** the final readable frame holds at least 1.5 s; a number with a short line needs at least 2 s.
- **Loop structure (6 s at 120 BPM, 12 beats):** the hook is already in motion at frame 0 (no fade from black). Build over beats 1–4, payoff on beat 5 (2.5 s), hold beats 5–10, then return to the frame-0 state on beats 11–12. Use periodic functions (`sin(2πt/T)`) so the last frame equals the first.
- **Sound to motion:** every discrete event (a click, landing, stamp or digit roll) gets a transient in the sound design and lands on a beat. Ambient drift carries the space between beats.

### 2.6 Restraint checklist
At most 3 elements moving at once. One accent colour. One material family per post (gold + onyx stone + optional frosted glass). A particle count you could name (12 motes, not 5,000 sparkles). No decoration that does not explain the idea.

## 3. Goldcom translation: 24 procedural concepts (three.js + Canvas 2D)

Palette tokens from `shared/brand.js`: onyx #151513, charcoal #1D1D1F, graphite #2D2E2B, gold #DBA761, bullion gradient, spark #F2D46B (accent glow only), espresso #3B2A1C (warm shadow), cream #FFF8EA (rare light frames), gainDark #3DBE84 and lossDark #F0605D (HUD chips only). Fonts: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Inter, JetBrains Mono for data labels.

### A. Price movement

**01 · Price Ridge: سعر الذهب اليوم** (6 s, 4:5 or 9:16)
- Idea: gold's recent path, today's price.
- 0.0–1.5 s: low-angle long lens skimming along a brushed-gold ridge. The last 30 days of price are extruded as a terrain ribbon with fog falling off into onyx.
- 1.5–3.5 s: the camera rises slowly; a single tiny 1 g bar slides along the crest toward the right end.
- 3.5–4.5 s: the bar settles on today's peak and a hairline price axis draws on the right.
- 4.5–6.0 s: today's price rolls in as an odometer (tabular, 160–200 px) with a tiny ▲/▼ chip in gain or loss colour. Hold.
- Technique: `ExtrudeGeometry` or `TubeGeometry` from the price series, anisotropic gold, top softbox plus side strip; Canvas 2D overlay for numerals.
- From: MVSM Robinhood (candlestick hill), Tendril Questrade (slab bars).

**02 · Slab Spiral: weekly recap** (6 s, 1:1 or 4:5)
- Idea: the week in gold, at a glance.
- 0–2 s: seven brushed-gold slabs in a shallow spiral. Slab heights equal daily closes, in darkness, lit by a moving light sweep.
- 2–4 s: the slabs light up one by one, Monday to Sunday, each with a soft tick on the beat.
- 4–6 s: today's slab glows from inside its seam with spark; the week's change % appears small in the lower third.
- Technique: `InstancedMesh` boxes, an emissive seam strip per slab, light-sweep uniform.
- From: Tendril Questrade spiral (`refs/tendril-2`).

**03 · The Number Is the Picture** (4 s, mostly 2D)
- Idea: today's price per gram.
- 0–1 s: the digits of today's price fill the frame (cap height about 45% of the frame) in onyx-on-onyx, readable only through a faint emboss.
- 1–2.5 s: a raking light sweeps left to right (RTL variant: right to left), turning each digit gold as it passes; digits change with an odometer roll where the price moved.
- 2.5–4 s: hold, with "ر.ع / جرام" as a small 28 px label.
- Technique: Canvas 2D digits rendered to a normal and height map, shaded in a three.js plane (or 2D with a gradient mask).
- From: DIA (type as image), BUCK "$150M" (stat scale).

**04 · Price Pulse HUD** (5 s, 9:16 Story)
- Idea: live price, live app.
- 0–2 s: macro of a polished bar surface (roughness 0.15); the reflection of a chart line slides across the metal like a reflected screen.
- 2–4 s: the reflected line resolves into a 1 px hairline graph floating above the bar, with one chip: "+0.6% اليوم".
- 4–5 s: hold, then loop.
- Technique: a dynamic Canvas texture used as an emissive card *reflected* in the gold (via the environment map), plus a 2D HUD overlay.
- From: MVSM HUD annotation, Tendril reveal-by-reflection.

### B. Purity (24K / 999.9)

**05 · The Pour: نقاء ٩٩٩٫٩** (7 s)
- Idea: 24K, poured pure.
- 0–2 s: a stream of molten gold, emissive orange at 1.5× intensity, enters frame top and fills a graphite ingot mould in macro.
- 2–4 s: the surface ripples, then settles; the emissive cools over 1.5 s to metallic gold (lerp emissive to 0, roughness 0.5 to 0.18).
- 4–5.5 s: a stamp descends and strikes on the beat, embossing "999.9". A 2 px camera shake decays within 200 ms.
- 5.5–7 s: hold on the cooled bar while the light sweeps the hallmark.
- Technique: a ripple-displaced plane, emissive-to-metal shader lerp, displacement map from Canvas text.
- From: Diptyque (show the making), Tendril Coinbase "LiquidGrow".

**06 · Four Nines** (4 s, macro)
- Idea: 99.99% pure.
- 0–1 s: extreme macro with the camera sliding across a hallmark plate in darkness.
- 1–3 s: a grazing light at about 10° hits 9 · 9 · 9 · . · 9, one character per beat (500 ms), each with a soft metallic tick.
- 3–4 s: a rack focus pulls back to show the whole bar. Optional line: "عيار ٢٤".
- Technique: an engraved normal map, a spotlight on a path, a bokeh DOF focus ramp.
- From: Tendril Hublot MP-10 (tease parts with texture), Studio River.

**07 · Solid All the Way Through** (6 s)
- Idea: not plated; gold to the core.
- 0–2 s: a bar rests on stone and a thin warm scan line descends through it.
- 2–4 s: where the line passes, the bar shows its cross-section: solid gold, finely grained, no core.
- 4–6 s: the section closes and the bar is whole again; a hairline caption reads "ذهب خالص".
- Technique: a clipping plane with a cap-fill shader (stencil) and an animated clip height.
- From: Tendril Coinbase R&D "MRI", Questrade "GoldTester".

### C. Starting small (1 g)

**08 · True Scale: ابدأ بجرام واحد** (6 s)
- Idea: you can start with just 1 gram.
- 0–2 s: macro, shallow DOF. A single Omani date rests on dark stone in a warm side light.
- 2–3.5 s: a real-proportioned 1 g bar (≈15 × 8.7 mm) slides in and settles beside the date (weight spring); the date makes the scale obvious.
- 3.5–6 s: the camera eases out 5%, a hairline dimension line draws along the bar, and the label "1 g" appears. Hold.
- Technique: a simple date mesh (procedural ellipsoid with a noise normal map and subsurface-ish sheen), exact bar proportions.
- From: Diptyque (a real object for scale), Tendril macro.

**09 · Grain by Grain** (7 s, top-down)
- Idea: small buys add up.
- 0–1 s: top-down on onyx with one 1 g bar centred.
- 1–4 s: it doubles: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 64. Each wave lands on a beat and ripples outward with a distance-staggered settle.
- 4–6 s: the grid resolves into the silhouette of a 100 g bar, or the Goldcom mark.
- 6–7 s: hold, line "جرام فوق جرام". Loop by fading the grid back to one bar in a light sweep.
- Technique: `InstancedMesh` (up to ~400), stagger by distance at 10 ms per unit, target positions sampled from a 2D mask.
- From: Optical Arts Dyson (mass arrangement), Ordinary Folk (one primitive as the world).

### D. Gifting

**10 · The Box: أهدِ ذهبًا** (6 s)
- Idea: gold makes the gift.
- 0–1.5 s: a black lacquered box on stone, lid closed, with a thin gold seam glowing faintly.
- 1.5–3.5 s: the lid lifts with the settle ease over 900 ms; warm light spills out and lights the underside of the lid and the surroundings.
- 3.5–6 s: a 5 g bar sits on dark velvet. The camera pushes in 4% and the light sweeps the bar. One line.
- Technique: emissive interior light, velvet via sheen on `MeshPhysicalMaterial`.
- From: BUCK (reveal through an opening), Giant Ant Koho (product reveal).

**11 · Pass It On** (5 s, 9:16)
- Idea: send gold to someone in the app instantly.
- 0–1 s: two frosted-glass slabs (abstracted phones) stand apart in darkness.
- 1–3 s: a small gold disc lifts from the left slab and travels a dotted arc to the right (dots appear along the path, DIA-style), landing with a soft chime.
- 3–5 s: the receiving slab's glass fills with warm light from the bottom up. Line: "أرسل ذهبًا".

- From: BUCK LinkedIn (hand-off metaphor), DIA (dotted paths).

## 4. Never do: the AI-slop tells

1. Kicker + headline + subline stacked on a dark patterned background, on every post.
2. Scattered sparkle stars, glints, lens flares, bokeh confetti or "magic dust" particles.
3. Gold as a flat yellow-to-orange gradient (#FFD700-style) with no reflections, environment or contact shadow.
4. Clip-art phones floating at 30° with drop shadows; flat cartoon gold bars with a highlight stripe.
5. Arabesque or geometric wallpaper behind content at 5–15% opacity. Negative space should be light, not pattern.
6. Several accent colours, several fonts or more than two weights in one frame.
7. Outer glow, bevel or emboss effects, or gradient fills on text. Letter-spaced Arabic. Per-letter Arabic animation.
8. Everything centred, symmetrical and the same size, with no scale contrast between hero and support.
9. Everything fading in together at 300 ms linear, elastic or bouncy easing on metal, idle sine-bobbing on every element.
10. Cameras doing 360° spins, or the default 50–75° FOV that makes products look like toys.
11. Inconsistent light direction between objects; objects floating with no contact shadow.
12. Stock metaphors: shield with tick, padlock, rocket, money bag, $ coins, 45° up-arrows, "to the moon" charts.
13. Charts that always go up, or growth visuals that imply guaranteed returns. Show grams accumulating, not profits.
14. Download badges, three CTAs or an app-store button on every frame.
15. Generated imagery with melted text, impossible reflections or wrong bar proportions (a 1 g bar is about 15 × 8.7 mm).
16. The same template every day with only the copy swapped. Rotate formats: macro, top-down, number-as-picture, linework, cel.
17. Emoji in on-image copy; exclamation marks; more than one line of display text.
18. Over-processing: heavy bloom haloes, chromatic aberration, vignette at 0.6 or more, film burns.
