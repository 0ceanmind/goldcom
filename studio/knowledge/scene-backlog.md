# Scene backlog

Concepts from `design-dna.md` §3 that are not built yet. When a brief needs one, the director builds it as a module (`create_scene_module`), tests it, and moves it to "Built" below. Each concept lists what the viewer sees and the one idea it carries.

## To build on demand
- **The Pour (05)**: molten gold fills a graphite mould, cools from emissive orange to metal, a die strikes "999.9". Idea: 24K, poured pure.
- **Four Nines (06)**: macro slide across a hallmark; a grazing light hits 9 · 9 · 9 · . · 9 one per beat, then rack focus to the whole bar. Idea: 99.99% pure.
- **Solid all the way through (07)**: a scan line descends through a bar and shows a solid gold cross-section (clipping plane + cap). Idea: not plated.
- **True scale (08)**: a 1 g bar settles beside an Omani date on dark stone; a hairline dimension line draws. Idea: you can start with just 1 gram.
- **Grain by grain (09)**: top-down, 1 → 2 → 4 → … 64 small bars ripple outward on the beat and resolve into a 100 g silhouette or the Goldcom mark (InstancedMesh). Idea: small buys add up.
- **Pass it on (11)**: two frosted-glass slabs; a gold disc travels a dotted arc from one to the other, which fills with warm light. Idea: send gold instantly.
- **Eidiya is gold (12)**: a cream envelope opens on espresso wood and three 1 g bars fan out. Idea: give gold as Eidiya.
- **Gold to balance (13)**: a bar dissolves edge-first into motes that flow into a balance figure on an app card (MeshSurfaceSampler → glyph points). Idea: sell any time.
- **Door to door (14)**: hairline map of the Muscat coast, a route draws from a vault pin to a home pin, a sealed pouch lands. Idea: delivered to your door.
- **Serial and seal (17)**: hairline measuring marks draw around a bar, a frosted certificate card slides over with a faint iridescent sweep. Idea: every bar has a serial number.
- **Portfolio monolith (19)**: a slowly orbiting stack of slabs, one layer per purchase; the newest slides on top and its seam glows. Idea: your holdings as one object.
- **UI exploded (21)**: the app screen separates into frosted-glass layers in depth as the camera orbits 25°. Idea: price, buy and vault in one screen.
- **Strike (22)**: a blank bar under a press; on the downbeat the mark embosses (normal map ramps in) with a 2 px shake. Logo sting.
- **National Day (24c)**: the outline of Oman draws as one gold hairline, then becomes an engraving on a bar face. Check the official date and anniversary number first.
