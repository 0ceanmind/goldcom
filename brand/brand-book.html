02 · Logo

The bilingual lockupالشعار ثنائي اللغة

An Arabic and Latin wordmark paired with an angular, gem-like "G" drawn entirely on a 45° grid. Always use the master files in brand/logo/. Never redraw or retype it.

  1. Arabic wordmark · جولدكمSits above the Latin. Arabic first, as the brand is.
  2. Latin wordmark · Goldcom"Gold" bold, "com" light. The contrast is part of the mark.
  3. Roof facetAmber #CD864B: protection and custody.
  4. G facetGold #DBA761: Goldcom, folded like a bar.
  5. The gemDetached diamond: the customer's own gold, held safely. It also defines the unit X.

07 · Imagery

The real object, in warm lightالذهب الحقيقي، بإضاءة دافئة

Show the actual bar, the phone that controls it, and the moments gold is given. Calm, editorial and one hero object per frame.

Show

  • Real bars and coins with legible weight and purity, and generic stamps
  • The phone with the dark Goldcom UI, held naturally or floating
  • Gifting moments: Eid, weddings, newborns, graduations
  • Omani and Gulf people in modest dress, respectful poses
  • Warm upper-left key light, golden rim light, charcoal, cream or Bullion backdrops
✓On brand

Never show

  • $ € or other foreign currency signs, coin piles, treasure chests, cash stacks
  • Luxury flexing, casinos, rockets, neon trading screens
  • Fake hallmarks, refiner, bank or regulator logos
  • Immodest dress, alcohol, AI artefacts, distorted engravings
✕Off brand

AI image prompt (product hero)

Photorealistic studio product photograph of a 5 g 24-karat gold bar with a clean minimal stamp showing only the weight and "999.9", resting on matte charcoal stone, warm directional key light from upper left, soft golden rim light, shallow depth of field, calm premium editorial mood, palette #62422A #DBA761 #151513, negative space on the left, no text, no logos, 4:5

Negative prompts and more templates are in the guidelines §7.4. Never let a model draw the logo.

09 · Voice

10 · Claims & compliance

Only what we can documentلا نقول إلا ما نستطيع إثباته

Trust is the product. Every claim belongs to a tier. Amber claims need approved wording and a document on file. Red claims are never made.

Use freely
  • On the App Store and Google Play
  • Omani, based in Muscat
  • Arabic and English
  • Start from one gram
  • Certified gold bars and coins
  • Track holdings and price history
  • Send gold as a gift
  • Sharia advisory by Eltizam
  • Identity verification and biometric login
Sign-off and document required
  • Insured (name the insurer and scope)
  • Licensed (authority and CR number)
  • Sharia-certified (certificate number)
  • Numbered bars recorded in your name
  • Vault custody (name the operator)
  • Delivery or collection (cities, fees, times)
  • 30 days of free storage (while live)
  • Buyback or settlement speed
Never
  • Guaranteed returns or profit
  • Risk-free or "safe investment"
  • Price predictions
  • Best, cheapest, #1, most trusted
  • Fully regulated, government-backed
  • Unverified customer, AUM or volume figures
  • Foreign-regulator references
  • Personal financial advice

Before you publish

The 12-point brand checkقائمة التحقق قبل النشر

  • Unmodified logo file, correct version for the background, 1X clear space, above minimum size
  • Name spelled Goldcom / جولدكم
  • Palette colours only. No gold text on light backgrounds
  • Spark is the single main action, ≤ 5%, with Ink text
  • Inter and IBM Plex Sans Arabic from the type scale
  • Arabic fully RTL. Logo, numbers and charts not mirrored
  • OMR with 3 decimals, tabular, labelled indicative
  • Claims are green, or amber with approved wording. No red claims
  • Disclaimer present wherever price, return, "invest" or "save" appears
  • Imagery: real object, warm light, no fake logos or $ signs
  • Voice: fact first, calm, short, no hype or urgency
  • Accessibility: AA contrast, 44 px targets, not colour alone
