## 0. Hard rules (never break)

1. **Logo:** use only the files in `logo/`. Never redraw, retype, recolour, rotate, stretch, mirror, outline or add effects to it. Never rebuild the wordmark from a font.
2. **Name:** write **Goldcom** in English (capital G only) and **جولدكم** in Arabic. Never "GoldCom", "GOLDCOM", "Gold Com", "Gold.com" or "ذهب كوم".
3. **Colour:** use only the hex values in Section 4 / `tokens.json`. Gold-family colours (Amber, Gold, Bullion, Sand, Spark) are **never used for text on white or cream**. Use **Bronze `#8A5A2B`** instead.
4. **Action colour:** Spark `#F2D46B` is for the main action on dark screens. Text on Spark is always **Ink `#000000`**. Spark covers 5% of a screen or less.
5. **Promises:** never promise or imply guaranteed returns, profit, "risk-free", "safe investment" or a price forecast. Never present gold as a way to get rich quickly.
6. **Claims:** use trust claims (insured, licensed, regulated, Sharia-certified, allocated in your name, vault, delivery) **only** as worded in the Claims Register (Section 11).
7. **Prices:** every price shown in creative is labelled as indicative or illustrative, in OMR with **3 decimal places**. Never put a real-looking live price in evergreen creative.
8. **Language:** Arabic layouts are fully right-to-left (RTL), with correct Arabic copy. Never put letter-spacing, uppercase or faux-italic on Arabic. The logo is **not** mirrored in RTL.
9. **Imagery:** never invent refiner hallmarks, certificate numbers, bank logos, regulator seals or other company trademarks. Never show dollar or euro signs, treasure piles, casinos or "to the moon" trading imagery.
10. **People and culture:** depict Omani and Gulf people respectfully, in modest dress. No alcohol, gambling, or content that is culturally or religiously insensitive.

## 1. Brand core

### 1.1 Who we are
Goldcom is an Omani digital platform for **owning real, physical gold from your phone**. Customers can start from **one gram**, see live indicative prices, buy certified bars and coins, and then **hold, track, gift, sell or collect** them, all in Arabic or English.

**One sentence:** *Goldcom turns a gold purchase into a real, documented holding you control from your phone.*

### 1.2 Positioning statement
For people in Oman who want to own real gold without the usual friction of buying, storing and selling it, **Goldcom** is the bilingual digital gold platform that takes you from your first gram to a documented holding you can monitor, gift, sell or collect. Unlike gold-price apps or paper-gold products, **Goldcom is about the physical metal**, with the ease of an app and the trust of a local service.

### 1.3 Brand promise
**Real gold. In your hands, from your phone.**
Arabic: **ذهب حقيقي، بين يديك من جوالك.**

### 1.4 The story we tell (narrative spine)
- **The tension:** Gold is familiar, valued and part of family life in Oman. But for a small buyer it comes with friction: prices change constantly, purity is hard to judge, storage is inconvenient, and selling can feel opaque.
- **The resolution:** Goldcom makes the phone the control panel for a real bar. Choose a modest weight, see the price, own a recorded asset, track it, and decide when to keep, gift, sell or collect it.
- **The feeling:** Calm control and reassurance. *"I know what I own, where it is, and what it's worth."*

### 1.5 Audiences
| Segment | Who | What they need to hear | Lead feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First-gram saver** | Young professional starting to save | "You can start small and own real gold." | From 1 gram, simple purchase |
| **Self-directed gold buyer** | Already buys gold, watches prices | "Transparent live pricing and clear exit options." | Live prices, charts, sell / P2P |
| **Family gifter** | Buying for Eid, weddings, newborns, graduations | "A meaningful gift that keeps its value, sent in seconds." | Gold gifting flow |
| **Recipient** | Receives a gift | "This is real gold, and it's yours." | Gift reception, collect |

### 1.6 Message pillars (use these, in this priority)
1. **Real gold:** physical, certified bars and coins, not a number on a screen.
2. **Accessible:** start from one gram, in your language, on your phone.
3. **Clear:** live indicative prices, fees shown before you pay, history in one place.
4. **Trusted and local:** Omani platform, Sharia advisory with Eltizam, identity verification and account security.
5. **Meaningful:** gold as a gift and a family tradition, made simple.

### 1.7 Personality
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Trustworthy: precise, factual, documented | Hype-driven or "get rich" |
| Calm: steady when markets move | Anxious, urgent, pushy |
| Clear: plain words, exact numbers | Jargon-heavy or vague |
| Warm: family, occasions, care | Cold or purely transactional |
| Local: Omani, Arabic-first | Generic, imported, Western-default |
| Modern: a clean, confident app experience | Old-fashioned jewellery-shop clichés |

## 3. Logo

### 3.1 Anatomy
The Goldcom logo is a **bilingual lockup**:
- **Wordmark (left):** Arabic **جولدكم** above Latin **Goldcom**. In "Goldcom", "Gold" is bold and "com" is light. This contrast is part of the design and must never be retypeset.
- **Symbol (right):** an angular, gem-like "G" built entirely on a **45° grid**. It has two facets: an **Amber roof** (`#CD864B`) and a **Gold "G"** (`#DBA761`), with a detached **Gold gem** at the base.
- **Meaning (internal use, do not claim publicly as official history):** the roof is protection and custody. The G is Goldcom, folded like a bar. The gem is the customer's own piece of gold, held safely beneath.

### 3.2 Asset inventory (`logo/`)
| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `goldcom-logo-horizontal.svg` | **Primary.** Light backgrounds (White, Cream, light photos) |
| `goldcom-logo-horizontal-reversed.svg` | Dark backgrounds (Onyx, Charcoal, Bronze Deep, dark photos) |
| `goldcom-logo-stacked.svg` / `-reversed.svg` | Narrow or square spaces: app-store creative, social avatars, signage, merchandise |
| `goldcom-logo-horizontal-mono-black.svg` | One-colour print, stamps, faxes, embossing, and light-gold backgrounds (Sand, Spark, bottom of Bullion gradient) |
| `goldcom-logo-horizontal-mono-white.svg` | One-colour on dark or photographic backgrounds where the two-tone symbol would lose contrast, and top of the Bullion gradient |
| `goldcom-symbol.svg` (+ mono) | Favicon, app icon, loading states, watermarks, small spaces under 120 px |
| `goldcom-app-icon.svg` | App icon master (symbol on Onyx, 62.5% height) |
| `goldcom-logo-original.png` | Reference raster of the official logo (do not use when an SVG fits) |

### 3.3 Clear space and minimum size
- **X = the width of the detached gem** (about 21% of the symbol's height). Keep **at least 1X** of empty space on all sides of any logo version. Nothing else may enter this space: text, edges, other logos or busy image detail.
- Inside the lockup, the gap between wordmark and symbol is fixed. Never change it.

| Version | Minimum digital | Minimum print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 72 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 20 px tall | 6 mm tall |

Below the horizontal minimum, switch to the symbol.

### 3.4 Placement
- Default position: **top-left** in LTR layouts and **top-right** in RTL layouts, aligned to the margin. Centred placement is fine for covers, splash screens and stacked layouts.
- Logo height should be roughly **4–6% of the canvas height** on posters and creative, and **24–32 px** in app or website headers.
- Use the logo **once per layout**. A faint symbol pattern in the background does not count as a second logo.

### 3.5 Background matrix
| Background | Logo version |
|---|---|
| White `#FFFFFF`, Cream `#FFF8EA`, Mist | Primary (black wordmark, two-tone symbol) |
| Onyx, Charcoal, Graphite, Espresso | Reversed (white wordmark, two-tone symbol) |
| Bronze Deep / top of Bullion gradient | Mono white |
| Sand, Spark, bottom of Bullion gradient | Mono black |
| Photography | Primary or reversed on a calm area; if the area is busy, add a 40–60% Onyx or Cream scrim |
| Amber or Gold flat fills | Avoid. If unavoidable, use mono black |

### 3.6 Misuse (never)
- Change or swap the facet colours (e.g. Amber G, Gold roof), or apply a gradient, metallic, foil, 3D, bevel, glow or drop-shadow effect to the digital logo.
- Stretch, squash, rotate, skew, or mirror the logo for RTL.
- Rearrange the lockup (symbol left, symbol between lines, Arabic below Latin).
- Use the Arabic or the Latin wordmark alone, or separate the gem from the symbol.
- Outline the logo, place it inside a shape that is not the approved app icon, or crop any part of it.
- Typeset "Goldcom" in Inter or any other font and call it the logo.
- Place it on clashing colours (red, blue, green), busy patterns, or low-contrast gold without the mono version.
- Animate it in a way that distorts its shape. The only approved motions are fade, scale ≤ 5%, and a single light "gleam" sweep.

> Physical premium applications (gold foil, embossing, engraving) may use the **mono** files reproduced in foil or blind emboss. That is the only time the logo becomes "metallic".

### 5.3 Bilingual and RTL rules
- **Arabic first** for Omani audiences. In bilingual layouts, Arabic sits on the **right or on top** and English sits on the left or below, with matching hierarchy (same size step).
- Arabic text: `dir="rtl"`, right-aligned, with mirrored layout, mirrored directional icons (arrows, chevrons, progress) and line-height about 15% taller than Latin.
- **Never** apply letter-spacing, uppercase transforms, italics or synthetic bold to Arabic.
- **Kashida (tatweel ـ)** may be used sparingly in campaign display headlines (≥ 40 px), as the official creative does ("تصـفح"). Never use it in UI or body copy.
- **Numerals:** use Western digits **0–9** for prices, weights, dates and percentages in both languages, as the app does. Always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for prices.
- **Do not mirror:** the logo, numbers, charts' time axis (time runs left to right in both languages), media playback controls, or brand images.

## 6. Graphic language

### 6.1 The 45° grid
Everything that is drawn comes from the symbol's geometry: **straight lines, 45° and 90° angles only**. Dividers, frames, arrows, chamfers and patterns follow this rule. Avoid organic blobs, swooshes, waves and circles as decoration. Circles are allowed only where function needs them: avatars, the central floating action button, radio buttons and chart points.

### 6.2 Facet pattern (`patterns/`)
A repeating tile of the symbol, used as a quiet texture:
- `facet-pattern-on-dark.svg`: Spark at 6% on Onyx/Charcoal
- `facet-pattern-on-gold.svg`: Espresso at 7% on the Bullion gradient
- `facet-pattern-on-light.svg`: Bullion at 10% on Cream

Rules: opacity is always **10% or less**. Use it on at most **one surface per layout** (usually the background behind devices or a hero). Never put it behind body text or tables, never on top of photos, and never in the colour of the logo at full strength.

### 6.3 Facet corner
A distinctive detail: a **45° chamfer** (cut corner) of 12–16 px on **one** corner (top-right in LTR, top-left in RTL). Use it for feature cards, price tags, certificates and gift cards. Use it at most once per view. Standard UI cards keep normal rounded corners.

### 6.4 The gem
The detached diamond from the symbol is a stand-alone accent: a bullet point in marketing lists, a "verified" marker, the centre of the app's floating action button, or a loading indicator (gem pulsing in Spark). Always use it at 45° and solid, never outlined, in Gold, Spark or Gold Leaf.

### 6.5 Iconography
- Line icons on a 24 px grid with a **1.75 px stroke**, rounded caps and joins, and simple geometric forms (Lucide-style). Filled versions are for the active state only.
- Colours: Stone (inactive), White (default on dark), Spark (active or selected), Charcoal (on light).
- Icon containers: 40–48 px rounded squares (radius 12) in Graphite, with optional subtle tints of Gain or Info for categories. Gold-coloured icons stay on dark backgrounds.
- No emoji in UI. Emoji are allowed sparingly in social captions (✨🎁 maximum one per post, never 💰🚀📈).

### 6.6 Data visualisation
- Price line: Gold `#DBA761` (or Spark for emphasis), 2 px, no smoothing artefacts. Area fill uses the chart gradient.
- Down periods use Loss with the loss area gradient. Up periods use Gain or Gold.
- Grid lines are Slate at 50%, and axis labels are Stone 12 px.
- Always show the time range (1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y / All) as a pill segmented control. The active segment is Spark with Ink text.
- Never imply prediction: no projected or dashed future lines.

## 7. Photography and imagery

### 7.1 What we show
1. **The real object:** gold bars (1 g to 100 g) and coins, crisp, with purity and weight legible. Stamps are kept generic, without invented refiner logos.
2. **The control surface:** a phone showing the Goldcom app (dark UI), held naturally or floating with a soft shadow.
3. **Meaningful moments:** gifting at Eid, weddings, a newborn or graduation. Show hands, gift boxes and family warmth. Faces are optional.
4. **Oman:** subtle local cues such as Muscat light, stone textures and traditional geometric patterns. Use national symbols (the khanjar emblem, the flag) only for national occasions such as National Day, and always correctly.

### 7.2 How it looks
- **Lighting:** warm key light from the upper left, soft golden rim light, deep but not crushed shadows.
- **Backgrounds:** matte charcoal stone, Onyx, cream linen, warm sand plaster, or the Bullion gradient.
- **Mood:** calm, premium, editorial and quiet. Use one hero object per frame and generous negative space.
- **Colour grade:** warm highlights, neutral midtones, deep bronze shadows. Never oversaturate the yellow.
- **People:** Omani and Gulf residents of all ages, in modest dress (dishdasha, kumma, abaya, hijab where relevant). Show natural, respectful poses. Hands holding gold should look natural and well groomed.

### 7.3 Never show
Dollar, euro or other foreign currency signs; heaps of coins or treasure chests; stacks of cash; luxury-car or yacht "rich life" scenes; casino or gambling cues; rockets, moons, bull/bear clichés or neon trading terminals; fake hallmarks or real third-party logos (refiners, banks, regulators) unless officially supplied; distorted text on bars; immodest dress; alcohol; obviously AI-generated artefacts (melted fingers, warped engravings).

## 8. Product UI system (dark by default)

### 8.1 Foundations
- **Theme:** dark by default (Onyx background). A light theme (Cream) is allowed for web, email and documents.
- **Spacing:** 4 px base, with the scale 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 64 · 80 · 96. Screen margins are 16 px on mobile, 24 px on tablet and up to 1200 px of content width on web.
- **Radius:** 8 for chips, inputs and badges · 12 for buttons and icon tiles · 16 for cards · 24 for sheets and modals · pill for segmented controls and tags.
- **Elevation on dark:** use surface steps (Onyx → Charcoal → Graphite) plus a 1 px inner top highlight (white at 4–6%), not heavy shadows.
- **Touch targets:** 44 × 44 px minimum.

### 8.2 Components
| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Primary button (dark)** | Spark fill, Ink 16 px SemiBold label, 48 px high, radius 12, full width on mobile. Pressed: darken 8%. Disabled: Slate fill, Stone label |
| **Primary button (light)** | Charcoal fill, Spark label (or White), 48 px, radius 12 |
| **Secondary button** | Transparent, 1 px Slate border (dark) or Mist border (light), text White or Charcoal |
| **Tertiary / link** | Spark on dark, Bronze on light, no underline until hover |
| **Floating action button** | 56 px Spark circle with the Ink **gem** icon, centred in the bottom nav, with spark-glow shadow. Main action is Buy |
| **Bottom navigation** | Charcoal bar, 5 items maximum, Stone icons, active item in Spark with label |
| **Card** | Graphite fill on Onyx, radius 16, 16 px padding, optional 1 px Slate border |
| **Product card (bar/coin)** | Square product image on a warm Graphite gradient, a weight badge (Info pill, top corner), name ("Gold bar · 1 gram"), purity line in Stone, price in White 700 tabular with OMR, round 32 px arrow button in Spark tint |
| **Price display** | Label (Small, Stone) → value (Price style, White) → change chip (Gain/Loss text on a 12% tint of the same colour, pill) → timestamp ("Indicative · 14:05") |
| **Segmented control** | Pill track in Graphite; active segment Spark with Ink text |
| **Chip / filter** | 32 px, radius 8, Graphite; selected uses a Spark 1 px border and Spark text |
| **Input** | 48 px, Graphite fill, radius 8, Slate border, focus 2 px Spark ring |
| **Badge** | 20–24 px pill, Caption 12 px. Status colours from the semantic set, "Verified" badge with the gem |
| **Toast / alert** | Charcoal, radius 12, left (LTR) or right (RTL) 3 px status bar |
| **Empty state** | Faint symbol (8% White), a one-line title, one sentence, and one Spark action |

### 8.3 Motion
- Standard 200 ms on `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. Sheets and modals 320 ms. Micro-interactions 120 ms.
- **Signature "gleam":** a single diagonal (45°) light sweep across a gold element, 1.2 s, used only for success moments (purchase completed, gift received). It never loops.
- Price updates crossfade digits. Never flash red or green backgrounds.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: replace motion with fades.

### 8.4 Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Use only the pairings in 4.4. Never rely on colour alone, so gain and loss also show + / − and an arrow. Support Dynamic Type up to 200%. Label every icon. Read prices in full in screen readers ("fifty-eight point four five eight Omani rials").

## 10. Voice and tone

### 10.1 Voice principles
1. **Say the fact, then the feeling.** "Own a real 1 g gold bar, recorded in your name." Then: "Start your gold story today."
2. **Exact over impressive.** Numbers, weights, steps and fees. Avoid superlatives.
3. **Calm about markets.** Prices go up and down. We never create urgency from price movements.
4. **Respectful and warm.** Address the customer as "you" (أنت). We are "we" (نحن). Use a polite, friendly register, never slangy.
5. **Short.** Sentences of 20 words or fewer, paragraphs of 3 lines or fewer, one idea per sentence.
6. **Arabic is not a translation.** Write Arabic natively in Modern Standard Arabic with a light Gulf warmth for social media. Always have Arabic copy reviewed by a native Omani or Gulf copywriter before publishing.
7. **British English** for English copy (colour, jewellery, centre).

### 10.2 Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Example (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign / ads | Confident, warm | "Real gold, from your phone. Start from one gram." |
| App UI | Brief, functional | "Lock price" · "Your gold" · "Gift sent" |
| Transactions | Precise, reassuring | "Purchase complete. 2.5 g of 24K gold has been added to your holdings." |
| Price alerts | Neutral, never urgent | "Gold is at OMR 31.204/g (indicative), down 0.8% today." |
| Errors | Calm, helpful, no blame | "We couldn't complete the payment. Your money hasn't been taken. Please try again." |
| Support | Patient, specific | "Here's exactly what happens next…" |
| Gifting | Warm, celebratory (lightly) | "A gift that lasts. Send real gold to someone you love." |
| Press / partners | Factual, measured | "Goldcom is an Omani platform for buying and holding physical gold." |

### 10.3 Vocabulary
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| real gold, physical gold, own, hold, your bar | digital gold token, paper gold, crypto-like terms |
| certified, 24K, 999.9, serial number | "purest in the world", "best quality" (unless substantiated) |
| live price (indicative), price lock, fees shown before you pay | "best price", "cheapest", "no fees" (unless literally true and approved) |
| save in gold, build your holding, store of value (with disclaimer) | guaranteed returns, profit, risk-free, double your money, get rich |
| secure account, identity verification, biometric login | "bank-grade", "unhackable", "100% safe" |
| gift, occasion, family | urgency: "Hurry!", "Last chance!", "Prices will never be this low!" |
| Sharia advisory by Eltizam | "Halal guaranteed" or "Sharia-certified" unless the certificate wording is approved (see 11) |

### 10.5 Microcopy standards
- Buttons use a verb plus an object: **Buy gold · Sell · Gift gold · Lock price · Collect · Verify bar**. Arabic: **اشترِ الذهب · بيع · أهدِ ذهبًا · ثبّت السعر · استلم · تحقق من السبيكة**.
- Sentence case in English UI ("Lock price", not "Lock Price"). No exclamation marks in UI. Use at most one in marketing.
- Always show what happens next and what it costs before a commitment.

## 11. Claims and compliance (mandatory)

Goldcom's credibility depends on making **only claims we can document**. Keep this register up to date. An agent **must not** move a claim to a more permissive tier on its own.

### 11.1 Claims register
| Tier | Claim | Approved wording |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Use freely** | App availability | "Available on the App Store and Google Play." |
| 🟢 | Local | "An Omani gold platform, based in Muscat." |
| 🟢 | Languages | "In Arabic and English." |
| 🟢 | Entry point | "Start from one gram." (keep in line with the current catalogue) |
| 🟢 | Product | "Buy certified gold bars and coins." · "Track your holdings and price history." · "Send gold as a gift." |
| 🟢 | Sharia partner | "Sharia advisory by Eltizam." / "بإشراف استشاري شرعي من التزام" (MoU announced 30 July 2026) |
| 🟢 | Account security | "Identity verification and biometric login." |
| 🟡 **Only with sign-off and a document on file** | Insurance | Name what is insured and by whom, e.g. "Stored gold is insured by [insurer]." Never "fully insured" without the policy scope |
| 🟡 | Licensing | "Licensed by [authority], CR [number]." Only with the number |
| 🟡 | Sharia certification | "Certified Sharia-compliant by Eltizam, certificate [no.]." Only with the certificate scope |
| 🟡 | Allocation | "Your bars are numbered and recorded in your name." Only while operationally true |
| 🟡 | Custody | "Stored in a secure vault operated by [operator]." |
| 🟡 | Delivery / collection | State the exact cities, fees and lead times |
| 🟡 | Free storage period | "First 30 days of storage free." Only while the offer is live |
| 🟡 | Buyback / liquidity speed | Only with documented settlement times |
| 🔴 **Never** | Returns | guaranteed profit or returns, "risk-free", "safe investment", "your gold will grow", price predictions |
| 🔴 | Superlatives | "best price", "cheapest", "#1 in Oman", "most trusted", unless independently substantiated and approved |
| 🔴 | Regulation absolutes | "fully regulated", "government-backed", "central-bank approved" |
| 🔴 | Unverified scale | customer numbers, assets under custody or transaction volumes not confirmed by management; any investor metrics or foreign (e.g. Saudi) regulator references from old website copy |
| 🔴 | Financial advice | telling a specific person to buy or sell, or when |

### 11.2 Mandatory disclaimers
Add the risk disclaimer (Caption size, Stone or Stone Dark) to any creative that shows a price, chart, holding value, return, or the words "invest" or "save":

> **EN:** Gold prices change and the value of your holdings can go down as well as up. Prices shown are indicative; the final price is confirmed at checkout. This is not financial advice.
>
> **AR:** أسعار الذهب متغيرة، وقد ترتفع قيمة مقتنياتك أو تنخفض. الأسعار المعروضة استرشادية، ويُثبَّت السعر النهائي عند إتمام الشراء. هذا المحتوى ليس نصيحة مالية.

On mock-up screens in marketing, add the small label **"Illustrative screen" / "شاشة توضيحية"** when the values shown are not live.

### 11.3 Privacy
Never show real customer names, balances, IDs or serial numbers. Use sample names (e.g. "Mahmood", "Aisha") and obviously sample values. Never ask users to share personal data in public comments. Direct them to support@goldcom.om or the in-app chat.

## 12. Agent workflow and QA checklist

### 12.1 Before creating
1. Identify the **channel, language(s), audience segment (1.5) and pillar (1.6)**.
2. Pick the **theme**: dark product, Bullion campaign, or light editorial.
3. Pull every colour, size and radius from `tokens/tokens.json`. Never eyeball values.

### 12.2 Before delivering, check every item
- [ ] The logo is an unmodified file from `logo/`, the correct version for the background (3.5), with ≥ 1X clear space and above minimum size.
- [ ] The name is spelled **Goldcom / جولدكم**.
- [ ] Every colour is in Section 4, and every text pairing is listed in 4.4. There is no gold text on light backgrounds.
- [ ] Spark is used only for the main action and covers ≤ 5% of the screen. Ink is on Spark.
- [ ] Type is Inter / IBM Plex Sans Arabic, from the scale in 5.2. Arabic has no tracking or uppercase.
- [ ] Arabic layout is fully RTL. Directional icons are mirrored. The logo and charts are not mirrored.
- [ ] Prices are in OMR with 3 decimals and tabular figures, labelled indicative or illustrative, with a timestamp in UI.
- [ ] Every claim is 🟢, or 🟡 with approved wording. There are no 🔴 claims.
- [ ] The disclaimer is present when required (11.2).
- [ ] Imagery follows Section 7, with no fake logos, foreign currency signs or AI artefacts.
- [ ] Copy follows the voice principles: short sentences, fact before feeling, no hype, no urgency.
- [ ] Arabic copy is flagged for native review if it is new.
- [ ] Accessibility: contrast passes, targets are ≥ 44 px, and nothing relies on colour alone.

### 12.3 How to report back
When you deliver work, add a short note: **theme used · logo version · claims used (and tier) · anything you changed from the request to comply · anything that needs human sign-off.**
